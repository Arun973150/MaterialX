# Research Dossier — Matching the Full Project Gist

Goal: build the **exact** project from the slide + gist — a generative-AI material-**discovery**
pipeline that outputs chemical formulas, **plus** a real-time **control** system that adapts the
coating to the environment. This dossier maps every step of the gist to the 2025–26 state of the art,
rates honest feasibility, and gives a concrete buildable architecture.

Feasibility scale: 🟢 buildable now · 🟡 buildable with caveats/data work · 🔴 aspirational / blocked by physics or missing data.

## TRACK A — Generative discovery (the gist, step by step)

### Step 1 — "Robust dataset of EM, optical, thermal properties" 🟡
- **Optical / dielectric / thermal (intrinsic):** real and available. Materials Project + JARVIS-DFT give
  dielectric tensors, refractive index, band gaps; recent GNN datasets cover frequency-dependent optical
  tensors (~1,432 semiconductors) and optical spectra (~944 materials).
- **Radar (GHz) absorption:** **does not exist at the molecular-structure level.** Every ML-RAM paper
  *generates* its data via EM simulation — e.g. ~460 FSS samples in HFSS ([arXiv 2502.17534](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.17534)),
  9,018 THz points in CST ([arXiv 2508.08611](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.08611)). Radar response is
  geometry-driven, not composition-driven.
- **Implication:** the gist's "dataset of molecular structures of radar-absorbent coatings" is half-real.
  Optical/IR intrinsic data → pull from DBs; radar → must be generated by simulation (our Phase 3).

### Step 2 — "Generative neural network trained on molecular structures" 🟡
- **SOTA (2024–26):** the field has matured a lot.
  - **MatterGen** (Microsoft) — SE(3)-equivariant *diffusion* model; generates stable, novel inorganic
    crystals and is fine-tunable toward property targets (chemistry, symmetry, mechanical, electronic,
    magnetic) ([arXiv 2312.03687](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03687), Nature 2025).
  - **MatterGPT** — transformer for **multi-property** inverse design ([arXiv 2408.07608](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.07608)).
  - **CDVAE / DiffCSP / DiffCSP++ / Con-CDVAE** — VAE+diffusion crystal generators, conditional variants
    ([review: npj Comput. Mater. 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41524-025-01881-2)).
- **Reality check:** these condition on **intrinsic crystal properties** (band gap, formation energy,
  modulus, magnetic density) — *not* on "radar reflection" or "coating emissivity." You steer them with
  **proxies**: high dielectric loss / conductivity (radar), specific n,k (IR/visible).
- **Implication:** usable to propose *new compositions per layer function*, not to one-shot a "stealth coating."

### Step 3 — "GNNs predict how new atomic arrangements interact with light and radar" 🟡
- **Light / IR:** 🟢 strong. Equivariant GNNs now predict frequency-dependent **optical/dielectric tensors**
  directly from structure — TSENN ([Nat. Commun. 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-69159-9)),
  GNNOpt ([arXiv 2406.16654](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16654)), dielectric-tensor GNNs
  ([arXiv 2601.04755](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.04755)). This is exactly "interact with wavelengths of light."
- **Radar:** 🔴 a GNN on a *crystal* can give low-frequency permittivity/conductivity, but the **device**
  absorption is geometry — it needs the EM solver (our ECM/openEMS), not a structure GNN.
- **Implication:** GNN forward model for optical/IR ✅; radar stays with the physics simulator.

### Step 4 — "Objective function to minimize reflection + thermal emission" 🔴→🟡
- This is the **core physical paradox**: strong radar absorption wants high conductivity; low thermal
  emission wants low-loss/controlled-emissivity photonics — opposite properties. **No single material does
  both** (the detailed spec admits this). Minimizing both in one composition is not physically achievable.
- **Implication:** keep it **multi-objective over a multilayer stack** (what our Phase 5 already does), not
  a single-material objective. The gist's wording is aspirational; the realizable form is the layered optimizer.

### Step 5 — "RL fine-tunes compositions for durability & weight" 🟡
- **SOTA:** deep RL for inverse inorganic design ([npj 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41524-024-01474-5));
  multi-objective RL balancing properties + synthesis cost; NSGA-II widely used.
- **Reality:** RL here is data-hungry and reward-design-sensitive; for fast physics, NSGA-III/Bayesian
  (our Phase 5) is usually more practical. RL earns its place for sequential composition building or when
  coupled to a learned surrogate.
- **Implication:** offer RL as an alternate optimizer head; NSGA-III remains the workhorse.

### Step 6 — "Output chemical formulas + manufacturing pathways" 🟡
- **SOTA:** synthesizability scoring ([ACS Omega](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsomega.2c04856));
  precursor recommendation from 30k+ text-mined recipes ([Science Adv.](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.adg8180));
  retrosynthesis ranking ([Retro-Rank-In, arXiv 2502.04289](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.04289)); LLM precursor
  prediction (GPT-4.1/Gemini, top-5 ≈ 67% — [PMC12754754](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12754754/)).
- **Reality:** "formula" → tractable; "manufacturing pathway" → research-grade, modest accuracy, **no
  commercial inorganic-reaction DB exists**.
- **Implication:** we can attach a synthesizability score + precursor suggestion to each candidate (honest
  confidence levels), which satisfies the gist's spirit.

### Steps 7–9 — Simulation stress-test → lab synthesis → feedback 🟢 / 🔴
- **Step 7 (virtual stress testing):** 🟢 **already built** — our Phases 2/3/6 (TMM + ECM + validation).
- **Steps 8–9 (lab synthesis + feedback):** 🔴 software can build the **active-learning loop**, but the
  physical synthesis/characterization needs a materials lab (out of software scope).

## TRACK B — Real-time control system (the slide) 🟡

This is the sensor→AI→coating loop. The pieces all exist in 2025–26 research:

- **Adaptive devices that actually switch:** NN closed-loop IR camouflage (3 °C/s, ±1 °C —
  [PhotoniX 2025](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s43074-025-00199-y)); VO₂ color-thermal regulator
  ([Light: Sci. Appl. 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41377-025-01968-x)); electrochromic IR emissivity
  contrast 0.69 over 2.5–14 µm; and — closest to all-band — a **coding metamaterial + IR electrochromic device
  with simultaneous IR tunability *and* microwave scattering reduction**
  ([PMC11501323](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11501323/)).
- **Sensing + classification + edge:** multispectral sensor-reconfiguration RL (ReSPEC,
  [arXiv 2602.10547](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.10547v1)); multispectral CNN fusion on NVIDIA Jetson
  ([IEEE 10137241](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10137241)).
- **Buildable now (in simulation):** sensor models → CNN scene/threat classifier → RL controller (PPO/SAC)
  → **use our physics models as the digital twin** of the coating. Hardware-in-the-loop needs the real device.

## Honest verdict

| Part of the gist | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Virtual simulation / signature-reduction model | 🟢 **Done** (our pipeline) |
| GNN property prediction (optical/IR) | 🟢 SOTA exists, integrable |
| Generative model proposing new compositions | 🟡 Real (MatterGen/CDVAE/MatterGPT) but conditions on *proxy* intrinsic properties, per-layer |
| Single material minimizing radar **and** IR | 🔴 Physically impossible → use multilayer (we do) |
| RL fine-tuning | 🟡 Real but data-hungry; NSGA-III usually better here |
| Output formula + manufacturing pathway | 🟡 Formula yes; pathway research-grade |
| Real-time control system (Track B) | 🟡 Buildable in sim + on edge; full loop needs the physical device |
| Lab synthesis + physical feedback | 🔴 Needs a materials lab |

**Bottom line:** the *exact* gist can be matched in **software** almost end-to-end — the only truly blocked
pieces are (a) a single all-band material (replaced by the multilayer we already optimize) and (b) physical
lab synthesis. The generative + GNN + synthesizability layers are real 2025–26 tech we can bolt onto the
working pipeline.

## Proposed buildable architecture (the realistic "exact" project)

**Track A — Generative discovery (extends our Phases 1–6):**
1. **Property dataset** — MP/JARVIS optical+dielectric (pull) + our EM-sim radar data (generate). *(have radar)*
2. **GNN forward predictors** — `matgl`/equivariant GNN for intrinsic optical/dielectric/thermal. *(new)*
3. **Generative model** — fine-tune **MatterGen** (or CDVAE/MatterGPT) conditioned on per-layer proxy targets
   (high-loss conductor for radar; low-emissivity / phase-change for IR; tunable chromophore for visible) →
   propose **new candidate compositions**. *(new — the gist's centerpiece)*
4. **Multi-objective optimization** — reuse Phase 5 NSGA-III over the stack; add an optional **RL** head. *(have)*
5. **Synthesizability + precursors** — score each candidate + suggest a synthesis route (LLM/precursor model). *(new)*
6. **Virtual validation** — Phases 2/3/6 + openEMS on cluster. *(have)*

**Track B — Real-time control:**
7. **Sensor models** — RGB/hyperspectral, thermal FPA, radar-warning, NIR. *(new)*
8. **Scene/threat classifier** — CNN (MobileNet/EfficientNet) + multispectral fusion. *(new)*
9. **RL controller** — PPO/SAC: scene → coating state, with **our physics as the digital twin**. *(new, reuses physics)*
10. **Closed loop + edge** — feedback + Jetson (TF-Lite/ONNX) deployment path. *(new)*

This is a multi-month program; the discovery track (A1–A6) is the natural next build because it reuses
everything we have and directly delivers the gist's "generate formulas → validate in simulation" loop.
